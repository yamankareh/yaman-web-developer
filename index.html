About Me


I’m a passionate software engineer at the beginning of my professional journey, with a strong interest in web development and creating modern, user-friendly digital experiences. I enjoy turning ideas into functional and visually appealing websites, combining clean design with efficient code to build experiences that are both useful and enjoyable.



My journey into web development started with learning the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Since then, I’ve continued expanding my knowledge and improving my skills by working on personal projects, exploring new techniques, and challenging myself to solve different programming problems. I also use Bootstrap to build responsive layouts and create websites that work smoothly across different screen sizes.



What motivates me most is the opportunity to learn something new every day. I believe that becoming a good developer is not just about writing code, but also about understanding problems, thinking creatively, paying attention to details, and finding better ways to bring ideas to life.



I’m always looking for opportunities to grow, gain practical experience, and work on projects that push me beyond my comfort zone. My goal is to become a skilled and well-rounded developer who can contribute to meaningful projects, collaborate with others, and build digital solutions that make a real difference.



For me, every project is a new opportunity to learn, improve, and take another step forward in my journey as a developer.
